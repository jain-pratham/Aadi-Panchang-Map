Aadi Panchang Map ikukusiza ukuthi ukhombe izinkanyezi, iplanethi, ama-nebulae, kanye nokuqonda izinkanyezi ngesikhathi sangempela.

I-Aadi Panchang Map ingumkhiqizo ozimele ovulekile (open-source derivative) osuselwa kuphrojekthi ye-Sky Map. Iphrojekthi yasekuqaleni ye-Sky Map yenziwa yaba umthombo ovulekile yi-Google ngo-2012. I-Aadi Panchang Map inakekelwa ngokuzimele.
