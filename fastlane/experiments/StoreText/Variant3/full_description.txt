<b>The original Aadi Panchang Map, kept simple.</b>
Aadi Panchang Map remains the cleanest way to explore the stars. Based on the original Sky Map project open-sourced by Google in 2012 and now independently maintained, it provides a fast, ad-free experience that focuses on what matters: the sky.

<h3>Key Features:</h3>
⭐ <b>Automatic Tracking:</b> The display moves with you, showing exactly what's in your line of sight.
⭐ <b>Expanded Knowledge:</b> Use the new info cards to get data on stars, planets, and nebulae.
⭐ <b>Small App Size:</b> Doesn’t hog your storage or slow down your phone.
⭐ <b>Open Source:</b> A community-driven project dedicated to free astronomy for all.
