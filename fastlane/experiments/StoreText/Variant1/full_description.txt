<b>Stop wondering what’s in the night sky.</b> Aadi Panchang Map is a fast,
lightweight planetarium that tells you exactly what you’re looking
at in real-time. Just point your phone at the sky to identify stars,
planets, and constellations.
<h3>Why you’ll love it:</h3>
⭐ <b>Point & Identify:</b> Use your phone’s sensors to track celestial objects instantly.
⭐ <b>New! Info Cards:</b> Tap an object to see its distance, history, and key facts.
⭐ <b>Search the Universe:</b> Looking for Mars? Let the app guide your phone to it.
⭐ <b>Pure Exploration:</b> No ads, no tracking, and no bloat. Just the cosmos.

Aadi Panchang Map is an independent open-source derivative of the Sky Map project. The original Sky Map project was open-sourced by Google in 2012. Aadi Panchang Map is independently maintained.
