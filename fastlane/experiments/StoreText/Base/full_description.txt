Aadi Panchang Map helps you identify stars, planets, nebulae, and constellations in real-time.

Aadi Panchang Map is an independent open-source derivative of the Sky Map project. The original Sky Map project was open-sourced by Google in 2012. Aadi Panchang Map is independently maintained.
